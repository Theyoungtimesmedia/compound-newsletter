
# Compound Newsletter Platform

Nigerian businesses have audiences. WhatsApp broadcasts. Instagram followers. Church members. Parent groups. What they don't have is a proper way to communicate with them without worrying about being flagged, hitting contact limits, or losing everything if the platform changes its rules.

Compound solves the hard parts.

## What It Does

An AI-powered newsletter platform built from the ground up for Nigerian creators and businesses.

- AI writes your newsletter — paste a URL or describe what you want. Done in seconds.
- Four email providers in rotation — if one fails, another fires. Your message always lands.
- Naira pricing — free plan, N1,000/month Starter, N3,000/month Pro.
- OPay and Paga payments — no dollar card needed.
- Live analytics — see who opened, what they clicked, when to send.
- Templates for Nigerian audiences — schools, churches, health coaches, creators, shops.
